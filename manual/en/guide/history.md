# About the Project

> "Software designed by an audio operator for audio operators"

## The Story behind LF Automatizador

**LF Automatizador** is not just a software project; it is the result of passion, resilience, and a love for radio.

My name is Luis Fernando Velásquez. I was an audio operator for 11 years at a community radio station in Venezuela, my homeland, which I had to leave to emigrate to Peru. Since I was a child, I lost my right eye due to congenital glaucoma, and in recent years the vision in my left eye has significantly declined.

I started this project without knowing how to write a single line of code. Relying on Artificial Intelligence tools, the Windows magnifier, and screen readers, I worked from an old computer, sacrificing hours of sleep and my own visual health. After months of trial and error, we managed to build a robust architecture, achieving the immense milestone of creating a professional audio engine.

## Why is it free and open source?

> *"The development of this program started from the beginning with Linux in mind, because I believe Linux needs more professional software focused on radio. However, along the way I realized that it would also be extremely useful for the Windows community, so I decided to make it cross-platform."*

I have decided to make **LF Automatizador** 100% open source because I know my visual health will not allow me to maintain it alone in the long run. If I keep total control, my passion will prevent me from stopping, and that will harm my vision even more.

I plant this seed in the Open Source world with the hope that passionate developers and programmers will nurture it, improve the code, and make this software the heart of many community and commercial stations in the future. All new development under this project must remain free and open to the community.
